# Capstone-Project
Project Title
Analysis of Supply Chain Delivery Performance Using Data Analytics to Reduce Late-Delivery Risk and Improve Operational Efficiency

Research Question
How can supply chain data be analyzed to identify the factors affecting delivery performance and develop predictive models that reduce late-delivery risk while improving operational efficiency and customer satisfaction?

Research Objectives
The primary objective of this study is to analyze supply chain data to understand delivery performance and identify opportunities to reduce late deliveries, improve operational efficiency, and enhance customer satisfaction.
The specific objectives are:
1.	To evaluate the overall delivery performance of the supply chain by analyzing delivery status, shipping duration, and late-delivery occurrences.
2.	To identify the key factors influencing delivery delays, including shipping mode, product category, order characteristics, and geographical location.
3.	To examine the relationship between delivery performance and business outcomes, such as sales revenue and profitability.
4.	To compare delivery performance across different markets, regions, and product categories to identify areas requiring operational improvement.
5.	To develop a predictive model for late-delivery risk using historical supply chain data and relevant operational variables.
6.	To assess the impact of late deliveries on customer satisfaction and overall supply chain efficiency.
7.	To provide data-driven recommendations that can help reduce delivery delays, improve logistics performance, and support strategic decision-making.
